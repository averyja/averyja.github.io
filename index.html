Cognitive Neuroscience · MRI · Decision Science

We study how the brain represents taste, food, and reward—and how those signals shape decisions.

Our lab combines multi-echo fMRI, quantitative behavioral tasks, and computational models to map how the insula and prefrontal circuits encode food properties, craving, and self‑control.

Based at OSU‑CHS in Tulsa, we collaborate across neuroscience, psychology, and public health to translate mechanisms into interventions.
